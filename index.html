01 — About

WHO IS INSPIDER?

I'm Fathiu Ayoade Adegoke, professionally known as INSPIDER, an esports organizer, community builder and gaming ecosystem strategist based in Nigeria.

My work revolves around one major goal: helping build a stronger, more connected and more professional African esports ecosystem.

Through community building, tournament organization, team management, partnerships and esports projects, I work to create opportunities for players, teams, guilds, creators and gaming communities.

I am the founder of the Naija Guild Leaders' Assembly (NGLA), an initiative focused on connecting gaming communities and guild leaders while creating opportunities for competitive gaming and collaboration.

I'm also involved in NGLA Esports and Noble Esports, where my interests extend into competitive team development, player opportunities, esports branding and organizational growth.

My approach to esports is bigger than simply organizing tournaments. Tournaments are only one part of an ecosystem — behind every competition are players, teams, communities, organizers, creators, sponsors, broadcasters and partners who need to work together.

My ambition is to contribute to an African esports environment where talent can be discovered, communities can collaborate and African players can compete with greater visibility and recognition.

I don't just want to organize tournaments. I want to help build the ecosystem around them.

NAIJA GUILD LEADERS' ASSEMBLY

Empowering Gamers. Uniting Communities.

Founded in September 2023, NGLA is a gaming community initiative focused on connecting guild leaders, gaming communities, players, teams and creators — built around the belief that gaming communities become stronger when they collaborate rather than operate in isolation.

06 — Flagship Tournament

NGLA UNITY SERIES '26

ONE REGION. ONE COMMUNITY. ONE TOURNAMENT.

🎮 Garena Free Fire ⚔️ Battle Royale + Clash Squad 📅 October 9 – 30, 2026 🌍 24 Teams · 11+ Countries

The NGLA Unity Series brings African gaming communities together under one competitive platform — giving established and emerging communities a shared space for competition, exposure, networking and collaboration. The vision goes beyond winning a trophy: it's about demonstrating what happens when African gaming communities stand together.

PHASE 1

Battle Royale

October 9–10

  • 2 groups · 12 teams per group
  • Top 9 from each group advance
  • 18 teams qualify for Phase 2
PHASE 2

Clash Squad

October 14–17 · 21–24

  • Swiss format · 18 teams
  • Maximum 5 games per team
  • Top 12 advance
PHASE 3

Grand Final

October 30

  • Battle Royale · 12 finalists
  • 8 maps
  • One champion

09 — The Bigger Picture

AFRICA SHOULD NOT ONLY PARTICIPATE IN GLOBAL ESPORTS.
AFRICA SHOULD BUILD ITS OWN ECOSYSTEM.

Africa has millions of gamers, talented players, passionate communities and emerging esports organizations. But talent alone does not create an ecosystem.

Sustainable esports requires tournaments, teams, organizers, sponsors, media, infrastructure, communities, player development and partnerships working together.

My long-term goal is to contribute to building that infrastructure — African competitions that are professionally organized, African teams with sustainable pathways, African players receiving meaningful opportunities, and African communities collaborating across borders.
